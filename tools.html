A collection of open interactive utilities designed for Sri Lankan university undergraduates, commerce students, and business owners.

Sri Lanka Tax

SL APIT Income Tax Calculator

Calculate Advance Personal Income Tax (APIT) based on monthly gross remuneration, relief allowances, and statutory progressive tax slabs.

Banking & Loans

Loan & Lease Amortizer

Calculates compound reducing-balance monthly repayments (EMI) and complete multi-year principal and interest schedules.

Billing & Invoicing

Instant Invoice Generator

Draft professional itemized client invoices with automated VAT/SSCL calculation and instant browser print/PDF export.

Financial Analysis

Corporate Ratio Analyzer

Evaluate balance sheet health across Current, Quick (Acid-Test), Debt-to-Equity, and Net Profit margins with risk indicators.

Personal Finance

Smart Budget & Expense Log

Manage daily income and expenditures with category tracking and automatic local persistent storage in the browser.

Trade & Customs

Customs Duty & Import Estimator

Calculates landed import costs into Sri Lanka with CIF value, Customs Import Duty (CID), PAL, and applicable VAT.

Real-time mock exam simulator for Business Fundamentals, Law & Taxation with bilingual question reviews.
